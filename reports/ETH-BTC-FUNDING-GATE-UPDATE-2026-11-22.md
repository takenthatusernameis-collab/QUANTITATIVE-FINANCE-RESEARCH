## Official schedule-change evidence

The current Binance Futures funding FAQ (updated 2026-03-06) states that the default funding interval is 8 hours at 00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC, but that intervals can be adjusted during extreme market volatility. [Official FAQ](https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/detail/360033525031)

The FAQ and announcements document materially non-8-hour schedules:

- **2025-05-02, 08:00 UTC:** settlement frequency can change from 8 or 4 hours to 1 hour when the previous funding settlement reaches the cap/floor; the new hourly schedule starts at the next funding time. Automatic cap/floor-triggered changes do not necessarily generate a separate announcement. The FAQ's BTCUSDT example uses a ±0.3% cap/floor. [FAQ section 8.1](https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/detail/360033525031) · [2025-05-02 frequency announcement](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/3243c81a35bd4f0c86a37315c3dc96cc)
- **2025-09-01:** an announcement describes reverting hourly funding to 4-hour funding after 36 consecutive cycles at an absolute rate no greater than 0.002%. [Official announcement](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/6d707b1f7ae34f419621b4c807464ab1)
- **2026-01-02, 12:00 UTC:** a later announcement specifies a different reversion rule: after 16 consecutive hourly cycles at an absolute rate no greater than 0.025%, the 17th cycle returns to 4-hour funding. The historical version/effective-date change itself must be reconciled. [Official announcement](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/e4445d0389ce4defa6009021fcf6ee46)

These rules do **not** prove BTCUSDT or ETHUSDT actually switched intervals at every possible time. They do prove that a universal 8-hour schedule is not a safe historical assumption. The observed archives' 8-hour-only cadence cannot certify a complete payable-event population for the full period.

## What the official API does and does not provide

Binance documents:

- `GET /fapi/v1/fundingRate`: historical rows with `symbol`, `fundingRate`, `fundingTime`, `markPrice`, and `rateType`; start/end bounds are inclusive, rows are ascending, limit is at most 1,000, and requests that exceed the limit return the startTime+limit slice. `Regular` is normal funding; `Special` is additional funding generated by stock dividends. [API documentation](https://developers.binance.com/docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Get-Funding-Rate-History)
- `GET /fapi/v1/fundingInfo`: adjusted cap/floor and `fundingIntervalHours` for symbols with adjustments. This is current state, not an effective-dated historical schedule. [API documentation](https://developers.binance.com/docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Get-Funding-Rate-Info)

A successful, fully paginated REST capture establishes what the endpoint returned; it does not establish retention/completeness for every payable settlement. The REST endpoint sample could not be retrieved through the available web reader in this run.
