### New hypothesis: CFTC leveraged-fund crowding and S&P 500 realized volatility

Preregistered and implemented `QFR-2026-11-25-CFTC-SP500-CROWDING-VOL-01`.

- CFTC TFF futures-only E-mini S&P 500 contract code 13874A; 1,067 reports from 2006-06-13 through 2026-11-17.
- FRED SP500 daily close; 2,512 observations from 2016-11-25 through 2026-11-23.
- Frozen 14-calendar-day COT publication buffer; documented 2025-09-30 through 2026-01-20 backlog excluded from signal and trailing-position history.
- Trailing 156-report absolute leveraged-fund net/open-interest percentile; high-crowding threshold 0.90.
- Baseline uses prior five- and twenty-session realized volatility; augmented model adds crowding percentile.
- Rolling 104-event OLS, strict mature-target rule, five-session forward realized-volatility target, no overlapping target windows, 2,000 circular-block bootstrap replications.

**Evidence-backed disposition: INCONCLUSIVE; experiment closed without tuning.**

| Forecasts | 116 | 197 |

- Test paired squared-log-loss 95% block interval: [-0.010763, 0.000095]; it crosses zero and the mean loss difference favors the baseline.
- High-crowding events had +0.2578 percentage points higher mean next-five-session realized volatility, but the 95% block interval [-0.2016, 0.6831] crosses zero.
- The augmented model did not improve the baseline in either period. The event-study mean was positive but uncertain, so the frozen rejection condition was not met and promotion criteria were not met either.
- No causal, executable-futures, strategy-P&L or live-trading claim is made.
