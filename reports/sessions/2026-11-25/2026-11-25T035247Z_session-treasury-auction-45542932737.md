## Research question and specification

Does primary-dealer accepted share add walk-forward predictive information about the next five-business-day change in a 10-year yield beyond bid-to-cover and the latest five-business-day yield move? The directional prediction is that weak auction demand (high dealer absorption and low bid-to-cover) precedes higher yields.

The baseline uses bid-to-cover and recent yield movement; the augmented model adds primary-dealer accepted share. Both use a rolling 52-event OLS window. Training observations are eligible only when their five-business-day target window ended strictly before the current auction `record_date`. Weak-demand scores use only the preceding 26 auctions and a fixed score threshold of 0.75. That threshold is not guaranteed to select exactly 25% of events.
